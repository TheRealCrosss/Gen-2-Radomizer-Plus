Gen 2 Randomizer+

Gen 2 Randomizer+ is a customizable randomizer and trainer rematch overhaul for Pokémon Recomp Gen 2: Gold. It expands the original Trainer Rematch mod into a full randomizer experience while keeping the rematch and Progressive Levels systems.

Randomizer features can be individually enabled or disabled through the custom Randomizer+ Settings menu, allowing you to change as much—or as little—of the original game as you want.

Trainer Battles

Random First Battle Pokémon randomizes the Pokémon used by trainers and Gym Leaders the first time you battle them while retaining their original levels.

Random First Battle Stats & Moves gives Pokémon in first-time trainer battles randomized stats and completely randomized movesets.

Random Rematch Pokémon allows trainers to receive new randomized Pokémon when you battle them again.

Random Rematch Stats & Moves adds randomized stats and movesets to Pokémon used during trainer rematches.

Progressive Levels makes trainers stronger each time you rematch them, gradually increasing their Pokémon's levels while maintaining their original level progression.

Rematch money and experience rewards can also be customized.

Wild Pokémon

Random Wild Pokémon allows wild encounters to include Pokémon from all 251 Gen 2 Pokémon, while retaining the encounter level appropriate for the area.

Random Wild Stats & Moves gives encountered Pokémon randomized stats and completely randomized movesets.

Starter Pokémon

Random Starters replaces the normal starter choices with randomized Pokémon, creating a different selection each playthrough.

Random Starter Moves can also randomize the moves your starter begins the game with.

Pokémon Randomization

Random Types changes Pokémon typings, allowing species to have completely different type combinations during the playthrough.

Random Learnsets changes the moves Pokémon learn as they level up, creating completely different progression and movesets.

Random World Items

Overworld Poké Ball pickups and hidden items can be completely randomized each playthrough.

Progression-critical items are protected. Key Items and HMs remain untouched in their original locations, and unused Gen 2 placeholder items such as TERU-SAMA are excluded from the random item pool.

Randomized TMs

TM moves can be completely randomized at the beginning of each playthrough.

Each randomized TM teaches a unique move, meaning two TMs will never teach the same move during the same game.

HM moves are excluded from the TM randomization pool, while progression-important TM02 Headbutt and TM08 Rock Smash remain unchanged.

Custom Randomizer+ Settings

Version 2.5 introduces a completely redesigned black and neon-green Randomizer+ interface built specifically for the mod.

Options are organized into easy-to-navigate categories:

Trainer Battles • Wild Pokémon • Starter Pokémon • Pokémon Traits • Items & TMs

Every major randomizer system can be independently enabled or disabled, letting you play anything from mostly vanilla Pokémon Gold with improved trainer rematches to a heavily randomized adventure where nearly every battle, encounter, item, and Pokémon behaves differently.

Every new game can be a completely different Pokémon Gold experience
